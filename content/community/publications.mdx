##  Publications 

Since 2003, Reactome papers have been cited in hundreds of textbooks and thousands of peer-reviewed articles and reviews, and the citation rate is increasing annually. Several papers cite the use of Reactome data for either integrative data analysis, algorithm development, or the inclusion of Reactome pathway annotations into a secondary bioinformatics database. A full listing of all the papers and books citing Reactome is accessible online at [Google Scholar](<https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=gtB1MLcAAAAJ>).
