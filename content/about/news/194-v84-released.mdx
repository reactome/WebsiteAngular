##  V84 released 

[Infectious Disease](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5663205>)

![](/uploads/about/news/infectious_events.png)

**New and Updated Topics and Pathways.** In V84, Topics with new or revised pathways include Developmental Biology ([Formation of definitive endoderm](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9823730>)), Gene Expression ([Formation of WDR5-containing histone-modifying complexes](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9772755>)), and Signal Transduction ([Regulation of TNFR1 signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/5357905>), [TNFR1-induced NF-kappa-B signaling pathway](</PathwayBrowser/#/5357956>), and [TNFR1-induced proapoptotic signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5357786>)). The [Infectious disease](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5663205>) pathway has been reorganized with grouping pathways for [Bacterial Infection Pathways](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9824439>), [Viral Infection Pathways](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9824446>), and [Parasitic Infection Pathways](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9824443>). 

**New and Updated Illustrations.** In parallel with the restructuring of the infectious disease pathway in V84**,** new or revised Illustrations with embedded navigation features have been created for [Infectious disease](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5663205>), [Bacterial Infection Pathways](</PathwayBrowser/#/9824439>), [Viral Infection Pathways](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9824446>), and [Parasitic Infection Pathways](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9824443>). Additional disease pathways with new or updated Illustrations include [Aberrant regulation of mitotic cell cycle due to RB1 defects](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9687139>), [Defective factor IX causes hemophilia B](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9668250>), [Defective factor VIII causes hemophilia A](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9662001>), [Diseases of Cellular Senescence](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9630747>), and [Diseases of Mitotic Cell Cycle](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9675126>). Normal pathways with new or revised illustrations include [Epigenetic regulation of gene expression](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-212165>) and [Gastrulation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9758941>).

**Annotation Statistics.** Reactome comprises 14516 human reactions organized into 2615 pathways involving 30095 proteins and modified forms of proteins encoded by 11371 different human genes, 14194 complexes, 2002 small molecules, and 1113 drugs. These annotations are supported by 36444 literature references. We have projected these reactions onto 84090 orthologous proteins, creating 19435 orthologous pathways in 14 non-human species. Version 84 has annotations for 4983 protein variants (mutated proteins) and their post-translationally modified forms, derived from 353 proteins, which have contributed to the annotation of 1,659 disease-specific reactions and 704 pathways. 

**Tools and Data.** Our services and software tools are designed for biologists, bioinformaticians, and software developers. Pathway data is available to view in our [Pathway Browser](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/>), to [analyze](</PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) your own dataset, to [download](<https://reactome.org/download-data>), and access programmatically through our [Content](<https://reactome.org/ContentService/>) and [Analysis](<https://reactome.org/AnalysisService/>) Services. The [ReactomeFIViz](<https://reactome.org/userguide/reactome-fiviz>) app and [ReactomeGSA](</PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) package provide tools for multi-omics data analysis. The [idg.reactome.org](<https://idg.reactome.org/>) Web Portal provides a collection of web-based tools to help researchers place understudied proteins in a pathway context. 

**Documentation and Training.** Visit our online [User Guide](<https://reactome.org/userguide>) to access documentation supporting pathway analysis of experimental data. The [Developer's Zone](<https://reactome.org/dev>) provides detailed documentation regarding our software, tools, and web services. Training and learning materials can be found [here](<https://reactome.org/community/training>).
