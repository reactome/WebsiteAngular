##  [ Version 62 Released ](</about/news/90-version-62-released>)

**[![](/download/current/ehld/R-HSA-445717.svg)](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-445717>)**

**New and Updated Pathways.****** In version V62, topics with new or revised pathways include Developmental biology ([Signaling by Robo receptor](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-376176>)), Gene expression ([Transcriptional regulation by E2F6 ](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-8953750>) and [Transcriptional regulation by RUNX2](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-8878166>)), and Immune System ([Interleukin-7 family signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1266695>),[ Interleukin-15 family signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-8983432>), [Interleukin-35 family signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-8984722>), and [Interleukin-38 family signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9007892>)). Illustrations are now available for [Aquaporin-mediated transport](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-445717>), [Cellular senescence](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-2559583>), [Epigenetic regulation of gene expression](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-212165>), [Gene Expression](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-74160>), [Negative epigenetic regulation of rRNA expression](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5250941>), [O2/CO2 exchange in erythrocytes](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1480926>), [Peptide hormone metabolism,](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-2980736>) [Positive epigenetic regulation of rRNA expression](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5250913>), [Post-translational protein modification](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-597592>), [Response to metal ions](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5660526>), [Signal Transduction](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-162582>), and [SLC-mediated transmembrane transport](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-425407>).

**Annotation Statistics.** Reactome comprises 11,302 human reactions organized into 2,176 pathways involving 10,878 proteins encoded by 10,712 different human genes, 1,768 small molecules, and 11,284 complexes. These annotations are supported by 27,526 literature references. We have projected these reactions onto 121,709 orthologous proteins, creating 20,854 orthologous pathways in 18 non-human species. Version 62 has annotations for 1,334 protein variants (mutated proteins) and their post-translationally modified forms, derived from 285 proteins. These have been used to annotate 506 disease-specific complexes and 906 disease-specific reactions organized into 453 pathways and subpathways, and tagged with 294 Disease Ontology terms.
