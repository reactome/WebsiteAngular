##  [ Version 83 Released ](</about/news/184-version-83-released>)

[Cytokine Signaling in Immune System](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1280215&PATH=R-HSA-168256>)

![R HSA 1280215](/images/news/R-HSA-1280215.png)

New and Updated Topics and Pathways. In V83, Topics with new or revised pathways include Developmental Biology ([Formation of axial mesoderm](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9796292>), [Formation of lateral plate mesoderm](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9758920>), [Formation of paraxial mesoderm](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9793380>)), Immune System ([Signaling by CSF1 (M-CSF) in myeloid cells](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9680350>)), Metabolism ([Gluconeogenesis](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-70263>)), Metabolism of proteins ([Insulin processing](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-264876>)), Metabolism of RNA ([mRNA Splicing - Major Pathway](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-72163>)), Programmed Cell Death ([Regulation of necroptotic cell death](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5675482>)), Signal Transduction ([Regulation of TNFR1 signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5357905>), [TNFR1-induced NFkappaB signaling pathway](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5357956>), and [TNFR1-induced proapoptotic signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5357786>)), and Transport of small molecules ([Zinc efflux and compartmentalization by the SLC30 family](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-435368>)). In continuing our collaborative drug annotation work with Caroline Thorn and [PharmGKB](<https://www.pharmgkb.org/>), we have added the pathway [Ciprofloxacin ADME](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9793528>) under the topic of Drug Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion (ADME) pathways.

[Cytokine Signaling in Immune system](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1280215>), [Diseases of DNA Double-Strand Break Repair](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9675136>), [Disorders of Nervous System Development](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9697154>), [Drug ADME](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9748784>), [Gastrulation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9758941>), [GPCR downstream signalling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-388396>), [Loss of function of MECP2 in Rett syndrome](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9005891>), [Pervasive developmental disorders](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9005895>), [Platelet activation, signaling and aggregation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-76002>), and [SUMOylation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-2990846>) have a new or revised Illustration with embedded navigation features.

Annotation Statistics. Reactome comprises 14,471 human reactions organized into 2,610 pathways involving 30,656 proteins and modified forms of proteins encoded by 11,442 different human genes, 14,153 complexes, 2,002 small molecules, and 1,113 drugs. These annotations are supported by 36,290 literature references. We have projected these reactions onto 83,933 orthologous proteins, creating 19,417 orthologous pathways in 14 non-human species. Version 83 has annotations for 4,977 protein variants (mutated proteins) and their post-translationally modified forms, derived from 361 proteins, which have contributed to the annotation of 1,659 disease-specific reactions and 704 pathways. 

Tools and Data. Our services and software tools are designed for biologists, bioinformaticians, and software developers. Pathway data is available to view in our [Pathway Browser](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/>), to [analyze](</PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) your own dataset, to [download](<https://reactome.org/download-data>), and access programmatically through our [Content](<https://reactome.org/ContentService/>) and [Analysis](<https://reactome.org/AnalysisService/>) Services. The [ReactomeFIViz](<https://reactome.org/userguide/reactome-fiviz>) app and [ReactomeGSA](</PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) package provides tools for multi-omics data analysis. The [idg.reactome.org](<https://idg.reactome.org/>) Web Portal provides a collection of web-based tools to help researchers place understudied proteins in a pathway context.
