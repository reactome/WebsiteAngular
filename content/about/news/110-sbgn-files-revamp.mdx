##  SBGN files revamp 

![SBGN Revamp](/images/news/20181004_SBGN_Revamp.png)

The [Systems Biology Graphical Notation (SBGN)](<http://sbgn.github.io/sbgn/>) project is an effort to standardise the graphical notation used in maps of biological processes. It aims to communicate biological knowledge more efficiently and accurately between different research communities in the life sciences.

Following this commitment, we’ve recently revamped our SBGN export methods and tools to provide more accurate representation of our pathway diagrams. Users have the option to either export a pathway diagram in SBGN through the [PathwayBrowser](</PathwayBrowser/>), or get [all human pathway diagrams](</download/current/homo_sapiens.sbgn.tar.gz>) from our [downloads section](</download-data>).
