##  [ New Paper published in Molecular & Cellular Proteomics ](</about/news/162-new-paper-published-in-molecular-cellular-proteomics>)

![ReactomeGSA](/images/news/ReactomeGSA.png)

A new research article entitled “[ReactomeGSA - Efficient Multi-Omics Comparative Pathway Analysis](<https://doi.org/10.1074/mcp.TIR120.002155>)” has been published in the [Molecular & Cellular Proteomics](<https://www.mcponline.org/>) journal. The paper discusses the novel ReactomeGSA resource for comparative pathway analyses of multi-omics datasets using our existing web interface and a novel R Bioconductor package with explicit support for scRNA-seq data. The ReactomeGSA tools are described [here](</userguide/analysis/gsa>). More publications from the [Reactome Team](<https://reactome.org/about/team>) can be found [here](<https://reactome.org/community/publications/>).
