We would also welcome reviews or revisions to Reactome pathways not on this list and contributions of pathways not yet represented in Reactome!

## _Questions?_

Email our team at [help@reactome.org](<mailto:help@reactome.org>) with any questions!

Here is an [FAQ](<https://www.reactome.org/community/collaboration/faq-for-prospective-reviewers-and-authors>) for reviewers and authors 

## 2\. Expert feedback

Conduct your peer-review by providing your comments and suggestions for improvement to our curation team. The peer-review process for a pathway is akin to conducting peer-review for academic journals. We will provide you with a text document, where you can provide your comments.

Our database updates are released quarterly, we are happy to work with you to find the best timeline for your busy schedule. 

[![](/uploads/community/Responsibilities.png)](<https://www.reactome.org/community/collaboration/faq-for-prospective-reviewers-and-authors>)

## 3\. Revisions

Provide your expert feedback to our curation team. Our curation team will take care of making your suggested changes.

## 4\. Public Release

You will be credited with authorship or reviewership for all of your contributions. Each pathway is associated with a DOI and can be cited as a publication.

You can quickly and easily claim your Reactome contributions in ORCID using our new ORCID claiming feature when you search for your name in Reactome as described [here](</documentation/userguide/claim-your-work>).
