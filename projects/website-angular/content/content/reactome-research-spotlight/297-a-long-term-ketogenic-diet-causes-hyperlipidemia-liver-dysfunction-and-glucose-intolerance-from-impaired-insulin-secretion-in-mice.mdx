## A long-term ketogenic diet causes hyperlipidemia, liver dysfunction, and glucose intolerance from impaired insulin secretion in mice - Reactome Pathway Database

In their September 2025 Science Advances research article, "[A long-term ketogenic diet causes hyperlipidemia, liver dysfunction, and glucose intolerance from impaired insulin secretion in mice](<https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adx2752?url_ver=Z39.88-2003&rfr_id=ori:rid:crossref.org&rfr_dat=cr_pub%20%200pubmed>)", Gallop et al. report multiple aberrations of metabolic parameters in mice fed ketogenic diet.

Reactome’s gene set analysis ([ReactomeGSA](<https://www.mcponline.org/article/S1535-9476(20)60015-9/fulltext>)) system is explicitly used for quantitative analysis of transcriptomic data derived from cultures of pancreatic islets isolated from male and female mice fed either ketogenic diet (KD), low fat diet (LFD), or high fat diet (HFD). [ReactomeGSA camera analysis](</PathwayBrowser?analysisTab=quantitative>) of differentially expressed genes in pancreatic islets of KD- vs. LFD-fed mice identified significantly up-regulated pathways related to [translation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-72766>), [metabolism](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1430728>), [Golgi-ER transport](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-199977&PATH=R-HSA-5653656,R-HSA-199991>), [coat protein complex II (COPII)-mediated vesicle transport](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-204005>), [protein localization](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9609507>), and [amino acid metabolism](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-71291&PATH=R-HSA-1430728>). Similarly, differentially expressed genes in pancreatic islets of KD- vs. HFD-fed mice indicate significant upregulation of pathways related to [vesicle transport](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5653656>), [ER/Golgi stress](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-381119&PATH=R-HSA-8953897,R-HSA-2262752>) and [translation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-72766&PATH=R-HSA-392499>). Targeted investigation of the expression of individual genes involved in vesicle transport between the ER and Golgi and to the plasma membrane, derived from [Reactome pathway gene sets](<https://download.reactome.org/97/ReactomePathways.gmt.zip>) and literature search, shows that many of these genes are significantly differently expressed between pancreatic islets from KD- and both HFD- and LFD-fed mice.

The study cautions that the long term ketogenic diet can have adverse systemic metabolic effects, questioning its use as a health-promoting dietary intervention.
