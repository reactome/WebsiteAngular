##  [ Nickel-induced transcriptional memory in lung epithelial cells promotes interferon signaling upon nicotine exposure ](</content/reactome-research-spotlight/248-nickel-induced-transcriptional-memory-in-lung-epithelial-cells-promotes-interferon-signaling-upon-nicotine-exposure>)

In the December 2023 issue of[ ](<https://clinicalepigeneticsjournal.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13148-023-01539-0>)Toxicology and Applied Pharmacology, [Zhang et al](<https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37951547/>). used the R package, ReactomePA [(Yu and He, 2016)](<https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2016/mb/c5mb00663e>), to identify enriched pathways responding to nickel-induced transcriptional memory changes in response to a second respiratory toxicant, nicotine. Nicotine exposure upregulated a specific subset of genes in the cells previously exposed to nickel, identifying a robust activation of [Interferon (IFN) signaling](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-913531>), a driver of inflammation associated with many chronic lung diseases.
