##  [ Genetic Networks of Alzheimer’s Disease, Aging, and Longevity in Humans ](</content/reactome-research-spotlight/229-genetic-networks-of-alzheimer-s-disease-aging-and-longevity-in-humans>)

Using Reactome analysis tools and FIVIz, [Balmorez et al. in the March 2023 issue of _Int. J. Mol. Sci._](<https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/24/6/5178>), established a commonality between the genes and pathways associated with Alzheimer's disease (AD), Ageing (AR) and Longevity. The pathways shared between AD and AR are [p53-Dependent G1/S DNA damage checkpoint](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-69580>), [FOXO-mediated transcription](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9614085>), and [SUMOylation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-2990846>); between AD and longevity are [Cytokine Signaling in Immune system](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1280215>), [Plasma lipoprotein assembly, remodeling, and clearance](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-174824>), [Metabolism of fat-soluble vitamins](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-6806667>), and [NR1H2- and NR1H3-mediated signalling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9024446>); and between AR and Longevity are [Immune system](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-168256>) and [Cytokine signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1280215>).
