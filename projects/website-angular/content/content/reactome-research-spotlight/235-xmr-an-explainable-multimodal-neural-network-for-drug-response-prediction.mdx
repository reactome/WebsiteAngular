##  XMR: an explainable multimodal neural network for drug response prediction 

In their paper titled “[XMR: an explainable multimodal neural network for drug response prediction](<https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10433751/pdf/fbinf-03-1164482.pdf>)” published in Frontiers in Bioinformatics in August 2023, Wang et al. use five Reactome pathways, [Cell Cycle](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1640170>), [DNA repair](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-73894>), [Disease](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1643685>), [Signal transduction](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-162582>), and [Metabolism](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1430728>), as an architecture of a visible neural network that is part of a deep learning model for prediction of drug responses in triple negative breast cancer.
