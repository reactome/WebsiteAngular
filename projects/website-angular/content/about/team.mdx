##  Reactome Team 

The Reactome group consists of an international multidisciplinary team from OICR, OHSU, EMBL-EBI and NYULMC, with expertise in pathway curation and annotation, software development, and training and outreach, dedicated to providing the research community with openly accessible biological pathway knowledge.
