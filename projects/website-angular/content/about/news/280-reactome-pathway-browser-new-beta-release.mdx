##  Reactome Pathway Browser: New Beta Release 

Reactome is excited to announce the beta release of our redesigned Pathway Browser, now available at: [](<https://dev.reactome.org/new-pwb/>)<https://reactome.org/beta/PathwayBrowser>

This major update delivers a modernized user interface (UI) and user experience (UX), along with new visualization and analysis features.

#### Key Highlights

##### **Event Hierarchy**

  * New hierarchical navigation panel for species, data overlays, and pathways.
  * Color-coded annotations highlight new or updated content.
  * Integrated analysis results directly into hierarchy view.

##### **ReacFoam (New Fireworks View)**

  * Replaces legacy Fireworks with a Voronoï-based map of pathways.
  * Pathways sized by molecular complexity and grouped by biological themes.
  * Stable, concept-driven layout across platforms.

##### **Enhanced High-Level Illustrations (EHLDs)**

  * Fully interactive high-level pathway diagrams.
  * Now powered by Figma icon library (free & open:[ ](<https://www.figma.com/community/file/1504140227575930077>)<https://www.figma.com/community/file/1504140227575930077>).  
Updated rendering improves analysis overlays and interpretability.

##### **Redesigned Pathway Diagrams**

  * Cleaner, SBGN-inspired design with consistent shapes and colors.
  * Integration of 3D protein structures (via AlphaFold, 3D-Beacons, PDBe) and ChEBI chemical structures.
  * Interactive legend and smoother zoom for detailed exploration.

##### **Analysis Tools**

  * Modernized interface with four streamlined modes:  
  

    1. Qualitative Pathway Enrichment (renamed from “Analyse gene list”)
    2. Quantitative Pathway Enrichment (renamed from “Analyse gene expression”)
    3. Species Pathway Enrichment (renamed from “Species Comparison”)
    4. Tissue Pathway Enrichment (renamed from “Tissue Distribution”)  
  

  * New sample-handling, filters, and improved visualization of enrichment results.

##### **Compare Mode (New)**

  * Side-by-side comparison of normal vs. disease pathways.
  * Interactive “layer” interface highlights disease-specific alterations.
  * Future-ready for orthology and other comparative applications.

##### **Search Improvements**

  * Unified results by reference entity (e.g., all forms of a protein).
  * Clearer separation between pathway-local and global results.
  * Direct navigation to context-specific appearances of entities.
