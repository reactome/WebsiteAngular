##  Version 82 Released 

[SARS-CoV-2 Infection](</PathwayBrowser/#9694516>)

![SARS COV 2 Infection](/uploads/about/news/SARS-COV-2_Infection.png)

**New and Updated Topics and Pathways.** In V82, Topics with new or revised pathways include Developmental Biology ([Epithelial-Mesenchymal Transition during gastrulation](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9758919>) and [Germ layer formation at gastrulation](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9754189>)), Disease-SARS-CoV-1 Infection ([SARS-CoV-1-host interactions](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9692914>)), Disease-SARS-CoV-2 Infection ([Induction of Cell-Cell Fusion](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9733458>), [Maturation of nucleoprotein](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9694631>), [Maturation of protein E](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9694493>), [Maturation of protein M](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9694594>), [Maturation of Spike protein](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9694548>) and [Translation of Accessory Proteins](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9727281>), Gene expression ([Transcriptional Regulation by NPAS4](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9634815>)), Metabolism ([HS-GAG degradation](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#2024096>), [Keratan sulfate degradation](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#2022857>), and [Transport and synthesis of PAPS](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#174362>)), and Signal Transduction ([Regulation of TNFR1 signaling](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#5357905>) and [TNF signaling](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#75893>)). In continuing our collaborative drug annotation work with Caroline Thorn and [PharmGKB](<https://www.pharmgkb.org/>), we have added the pathways [Prednisone ADME](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9757110>) and [Ribavirin ADME](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9755088>) under the topic of Drug Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, and Excretion (ADME) pathways. 

**New and Updated Illustrations.** [Drug Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism and Excretion (ADME)](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9748784>), [Gastrulation](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9758941>), [SARS-CoV-2 infection](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#9694516>), [SUMOylation](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#2990846>), and [SUMO E3 ligases SUMOylate target proteins](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#3108232>) have a new or revised Illustration with embedded navigation features. The updated SARS-CoV-2 illustration featured above highlights the recent partitioning of the pathway into early and late subpathways based on our growing understanding of the life cycle of this virus.

**Thanks to our Contributors.** [David Hill](<https://orcid.org/0000-0001-7476-6306>), [Yingxi Lin](<https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7002-1275>), [Rainer de Martin](<https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6950-1634>), [Caroline Thorn](<https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5789-5747>), and [Hailin Tu](<https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9362-4343>) are our external reviewers.

**Annotation Statistics.** Reactome comprises 14398 human reactions organized into 2601 pathways involving 11393 proteins and modified forms of proteins encoded by 11097 different human genes, 14084 complexes, 1998 small molecules, and 1035 drugs. These annotations are supported by 35965 literature references. We have projected these reactions onto 83288 orthologous proteins, creating 19385 orthologous pathways in 15 non-human species. Version 82 has annotations for 4977 protein variants (mutated proteins) and their post-translationally modified forms, derived from 352 proteins, which have contributed to the annotation of 1659 disease-specific reactions and 704 pathways.

**Tools and Data.** Our services and software tools are designed for biologists, bioinformaticians, and software developers. Pathway data is available to view in our [Pathway Browser](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/>), to [analyze](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) your own dataset, to [download](<https://reactome.org/download-data>), and access programmatically through our [Content](<https://reactome.org/ContentService/>) and Services. The [ReactomeFIViz](<https://reactome.org/userguide/reactome-fiviz>) app and [ReactomeGSA](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) package provide tools for multi-omics data analysis. The [idg.reactome.org](<https://idg.reactome.org/>) Web Portal provides a collection of web-based tools to help researchers place understudied proteins in a pathway context. 

**Documentation and Training.** Visit our online [User Guide](<https://reactome.org/userguide>) to access documentation supporting pathway analysis of experimental data. The [Developer's Zone](<https://reactome.org/dev>) provides detailed documentation regarding our software, tools, and web services. Training and learning materials can be found [here](<https://reactome.org/community/training>)..
