## V97 Released

![](</images/Adaptive_Immune_System_R-HSA-1280218.png>)

[Adaptive Immune System](</PathwayBrowser/#1280218>)

New and Updated Topics and Pathways. Topics with new or revised pathways in this release include Chromatin organization ([SWI/SNF chromatin remodelers](</PathwayBrowser/#9932451>)), Developmental biology ([Developmental lineage of pancreatic endocrine mid progenitor cells](</PathwayBrowser/#9981148>) and [Differentiation of circulating monocytes](</PathwayBrowser/#9968734>)), DNA repair ([Negative regulation of DNA double strand break response](</PathwayBrowser/#9974237>) and [Recruitment and ATM-mediated phosphorylation of repair and signaling proteins at DNA double strand breaks](</PathwayBrowser/#5693565>)), Gene expression ([RNA polymerase I transcription](</PathwayBrowser/#73864>)), Immune system ([Affinity selection of immunoglobulins](</PathwayBrowser/#9938027>), [IFIT antiviral response](</PathwayBrowser/#9684482>), [Immunoglobulin maturation](</PathwayBrowser/#9938026>), and [Somatic hypermutation of immunoglobulins](</PathwayBrowser/#9938024>)),  Metabolism ([Activation of gene expression by SREBF (SREBP)](</PathwayBrowser/#2426168>), [ChREBP activates metabolic gene expression](</PathwayBrowser/#163765>), and  [Urea cycle](</PathwayBrowser/#70635>)), Organelle biogenesis and maintenance ([Assembly of the 9+2 motile cilia](</PathwayBrowser/#9975924>) and [Transcriptional regulation of multiciliogenesis](</PathwayBrowser/#9945556>)), Signal Transduction ([PIP3 activates AKT signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#1257604>)).

New and Updated Illustrations.  New or revised illustrations with embedded navigation features have been created for  [Adaptive Immune System](</PathwayBrowser/#1280218>), [Antiviral mechanism by IFN-stimulated genes](</PathwayBrowser/#1169410>), [Cilium Assembly](</PathwayBrowser/#5617833>),  [Assembly of the 9+2 motile cilium](</PathwayBrowser/#9975924>),  [Defective FV causes thrombophilia](</PathwayBrowser/#9930483>),   [Developmental Cell Lineages](</PathwayBrowser/#9734767>),  and [Immunoglobulin maturation](</PathwayBrowser/#9938026>).

Annotation Statistics. Reactome comprises 16,423 human reactions organized into 2,883 pathways involving 32,486 proteins and modified forms of proteins encoded by 11,469 different human genes, 16,241 complexes, 2,188 small molecules, and 1102 drugs. These annotations are supported by 43,308 literature references. We have projected these reactions onto 83,396 orthologous proteins, creating 20,708 orthologous pathways in 14 non-human species. Version 97 has annotations for 5,752 protein variants (mutated proteins) and their post-translationally modified forms, derived from 400  proteins, which have contributed to the annotation of 2,060 disease-specific reactions and 789 pathways. Our Illustration library now includes 2,500 icons and over 200 high level interactive pathway Illustrations.

About the Reactome Project. Reactome is a collaboration between groups at the Ontario Institute for Cancer Research, Oregon Health and Science University, New York University Langone Medical Center, and The EMBL - European Bioinformatics Institute. Reactome is both an [ELIXIR Core Data Resource](<https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/data/core-data-resources>) and a [Global Core Biodata Resource](<https://globalbiodata.org/scientific-activities/global-core-biodata-resources/>) and has been certified as a Trustworthy Data Repository by the [CoreTrustSeal](<https://www.coretrustseal.org/>) Standards and Certification Board.
