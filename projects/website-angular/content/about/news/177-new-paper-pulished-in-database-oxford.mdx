##  New Paper pulished in Database (Oxford) 

![baac009f1](/uploads/about/news/baac009f1.jpeg)

A new research article entitled “[Evaluating the predictive accuracy of curated biological pathways in a public knowledgebase](<https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baac009>)” has been published in the [Database (Oxford)](<https://academic.oup.com/database>) journal. The paper describes the utility of Reactome pathways for predicting functional consequences of genetic perturbations. Predictions of perturbation effects based on Reactome pathways were compared against published empirical observations. More publications from the [Reactome Team](<https://reactome.org/about/team>) can be found [here](<https://reactome.org/community/publications/>).
