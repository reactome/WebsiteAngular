## V98 Released

![Developmental Cell Lineages of the Endocrine Pancreas](<https://download.reactome.org/98/ehld/R-HSA-9820460.svg>)

[Developmental Cell Lineages of the Endocrine Pancreas](</PathwayBrowser/#9820460>)

New and Updated Topics and Pathways. Topics with new or revised pathways in this release include [Regulation of the circadian clock](</PathwayBrowser/#9859713>) ([AMP and AMPK regulate the circadian clock](</PathwayBrowser/#9983885>) and [NAD+ and sirtuins regulate the circadian clock](</PathwayBrowser/#9958957>)), Developmental biology ([Developmental Cell Lineages of the Endocrine Pancreas](</PathwayBrowser/#9820460>), [Developmental lineage of pancreatic beta/delta late endocrine progenitors](</PathwayBrowser/#9985087>), and [Sprouting angiogenesis](</PathwayBrowser/#9917416>)), Gene expression ([Mitochondrial transcription](</PathwayBrowser/#9985686>), [PRC2 methylates histones and DNA](</PathwayBrowser/#212300>), and [RUNX1 interacts with co-factors whose precise effect on RUNX1 targets is not known](</PathwayBrowser/#8939243>)), Immune system ([Antigen activates B Cell Receptor (BCR) leading to generation of second messengers](</PathwayBrowser/#983695>), [IFIT antiviral response](</PathwayBrowser/#9684482>), [OAS antiviral response](</PathwayBrowser/#8983711>), and [Rituximab:MS4A1 (rituximab:CD20) targets B cells for antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity by NK cells](</PathwayBrowser/#9992296>)), and Protein localization ([Cotranslational targeting of proteins to the endoplasmic reticulum](</PathwayBrowser/#9986646>)).

New and Updated Illustrations. New or revised illustrations with embedded navigation features have been created for [Circadian clock](</PathwayBrowser/#9909396>), [Developmental Biology](</PathwayBrowser/#1266738>), [Developmental Cell Lineages](</PathwayBrowser/#9734767>), [Developmental Cell Lineages of the Endocrine Pancreas](</PathwayBrowser/#9820460>), [Gene expression (Transcription)](</PathwayBrowser/#74160>), [Protein localization](</PathwayBrowser/#9609507>), [Regulation of the circadian clock](</PathwayBrowser/#9859713>), and [Translation](</PathwayBrowser/#72766>).

Annotation Statistics. Reactome comprises 16,503 human reactions organized into 2,889 pathways involving 32,565 proteins and modified forms of proteins encoded by 11,496 different human genes, 16,325 complexes, 2,178 small molecules, and 1,103 drugs. These annotations are supported by 43,490 literature references. We have projected these reactions onto 83,732 orthologous proteins, creating 20,751 orthologous pathways in 14 non-human species. Version 98 has annotations for 5,752 protein variants and their post-translationally modified forms in different localizations, derived from 400 proteins, which have contributed to the annotation of 2,060 disease-specific reactions and 789 pathways.
