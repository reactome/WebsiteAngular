##  [ ReacFoam: Genome-wide pathway overview based on Voronoi tessellation ](</about/news/141-reacfoam-genome-wide-pathway-overview-based-on-voronoi-tessellation>)

[![](/uploads/about/news/voroni.png)](<https://reactome.org/reacfoam/?species=48887&color=COPPER%20PLUS&analysis=MjAxOTA5MjMxMDAwNDBfNTczMQ==&resource=TOTAL>)

As part of our continuous effort to provide visually attractive and more user-friendly access to our biological pathways, we are pleased to announce our new high level pathways overview visualisation based on Voronoi tessellation.

Following any [pathway analysis](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>), the [pathway overview](<https://reactome.org/reacfoam/?species=48887&color=COPPER%20PLUS&analysis=MjAxOTA2MjAxMjE0NTFfMTM0&resource=TOTAL>) provides a [new icon![](/uploads/about/news/Screenshot_2019-07-02_at_124156.png)](</reacfoam>) which leads to a comprehensive, highly visual, interactive overview of pathway analysis results. The functionality is available on both desktop and mobile platforms.
