##  V85 released 

![R HSA 9758941](/uploads/about/news/R-HSA-9758941.png)

**New and Updated Topics and Pathways.** In V85, Topics with new or revised pathways include Developmental Biology ([Cardiogenesis](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9733709>), [Formation of intermediate mesoderm](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9761174>), [Formation of paraxial mesoderm](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9793380>), [Somitogenesis](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9824272>), [Specification of primordial germ cells](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9827857>) ), Cell-Cell communication ([Regulation of Expression and Function of Type II Classical Cadherins](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9764260>), [Regulation of CDH11 Expression and Function](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9759475>), and [Regulation of CDH19 Expression and Function](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9764302>)), Cellular responses to stimuli ([ATF6B (ATF6-beta) activates chaperones](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-8874177>)), Immune System ([IFNG signaling activates MAPKs](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9732724>) and [Interferon gamma signaling](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-877300>)), and Metabolism ([Fructose biosynthesis](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-5652227>) and [Fructose catabolism](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-70350>)). 

**New and Updated Illustrations.** New or revised Illustrations with embedded navigation features have been created for [Developmental Biology](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1266738>), [Gastrulation](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-9758941>), and [Reproduction](</PathwayBrowser/#/R-HSA-1474165>).

**Annotation Statistics****.** Reactome comprises 14,628 human reactions organized into 2,629 **** pathways involving 30,155 proteins and modified forms of proteins encoded by 11,396 different human genes, 14,277 complexes, 2,004 small molecules, and 1,114 drugs. These annotations are supported by 36,706 literature references. We have projected these reactions onto 84,167 orthologous proteins, creating 19,505 orthologous pathways in 14 non-human species. Version 85 has annotations for 4,919 protein variants (mutated proteins) and their post-translationally modified forms, derived from 354 proteins, which have contributed to the annotation of 1,659 disease-specific reactions and 707 pathways. 

**Tools and Data.** Our services and software tools are designed for biologists, bioinformaticians, and software developers. Pathway data is available to view in our [Pathway Browser](<https://reactome.org/PathwayBrowser/>), to [analyze](</PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) your own dataset, to [download](<https://reactome.org/download-data>), and access programmatically through our [Content](<https://reactome.org/ContentService/>) and [Analysis](<https://reactome.org/AnalysisService/>) Services. The [ReactomeFIViz](<https://reactome.org/userguide/reactome-fiviz>) app and [ReactomeGSA](</PathwayBrowser/#TOOL=AT>) package provide tools for multi-omics data analysis. The [idg.reactome.org](<https://idg.reactome.org/>) Web Portal provides a collection of web-based tools to help researchers place understudied proteins in a pathway context. 

**Documentation and Training.** Visit our online [User Guide](<https://reactome.org/userguide>) to access documentation supporting pathway analysis of experimental data. The [Developer's Zone](<https://reactome.org/dev>) provides detailed documentation regarding our software, tools, and web services. Training and learning materials can be found [here](<https://reactome.org/community/training>).
