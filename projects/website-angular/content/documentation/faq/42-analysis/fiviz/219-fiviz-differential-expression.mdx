##  Can I submit a list of differentially expressed genes, as log2 fold change values, coming from two different conditions to have a quantitative representation of pathways involvement? 

If you want to use your log fold change data directly, you may use the Reactome Cytoscape app, described [here](</userguide/reactome-fiviz>) (search for “Perform GSEA analysis”). This feature will sort your genes based on log fold change and then calculate a score for each pathway using GSEA’s ranked gene list input.
